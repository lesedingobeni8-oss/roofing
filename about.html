About Bhorholo Roofing

A local roofing and construction company serving KwaMhlanga, Mandela and surrounding Mpumalanga areas.

Making customers feel at home

Bhorholo Roofing PTY LTD focuses on practical roofing and construction solutions for residential and property projects. The goal is simple: communicate clearly, do quality work and leave customers with a finished result they can be proud of.

Our services are suited to new homes, extensions, renovations, roof replacements, repairs and related construction needs.

  • Local team serving the KwaMhlanga area
  • Roofing for new builds and existing properties
  • Trusses, tiles, metal sheeting and repairs
  • Customer-focused communication
